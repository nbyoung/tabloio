usage: tabloio [-C <dir>] <command> [<options>]

Read commands write one view and change nothing in the project.

  gates         What do the columns and symbols mean?
  task <id>     What is this task and where does it stand?
  authority     Who may accept what?
  assignment    What does each person carry?
  queue         What do I do next?
  blockage      What waits on what?
  tableau       How does the whole project stand?
  context       How does my corner stand?
  history       What happened, when, and who did it?
  audit         Where do files and history disagree?

Other commands.

  version       Print the version
  help          Print this text, or the options of one command

Run 'tabloio <command> --help' for the options of one command.
